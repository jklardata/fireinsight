Common questions
What data do I need to get started?
An incident export from your RMS: ESO, ImageTrend, FIREHOUSE, FirstDue, or a NERIS export. If your department is already in our system from NFIRS 2024, you may not need to upload anything at all. Check your department.
How does the NERIS Compliance Check work?
It's free and takes about 30 seconds. We check your records against all 6 mandatory NERIS modules and tell you exactly which fields are missing or incomplete, in plain English. No technical knowledge required.
What format are the reports delivered in?
A formatted document emailed directly to you, ready to present. Board reports are formatted for reading aloud or sharing as a PDF. Grant narratives are formatted to copy directly into the FEMA application portal.
Can I use report credits for any report type?
Yes. The $99 credit pack gives you 6 credits usable on any report. A Board Report or Trend Summary costs 1 credit. Staffing Justification and ISO Evidence Pack cost 3 credits each. A Grant Narrative costs 5 credits.
Do credits expire?
No. Use them whenever you need them — budget season, grant cycles, board meetings. There's no expiration date.
Is there a subscription option?
Not currently. Pay-per-report works better for most departments that generate reports a few times per year, not every week. If your department needs monthly reports regularly, email us and we'll work something out.
